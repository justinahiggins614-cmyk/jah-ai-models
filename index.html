How to use the Signature AI Phone Book

Every AI in the book has its own phone number, its own file, and its own voice. Here is everything the site does, in plain language.

Search the book

The search box at the top finds any AI by name, ID stamp (like JAH-AI-SIG-001), phone number (digits or 1-200-0000001 form), or a plain description of what you need. Type 2 or more letters and tap a result — Enter opens the first match.

A–Z directory

The thumb index jumps to any letter page of the book. Letter pages remember which ones you left open. The “Search the book” box inside filters listings live.

Finder — ask in plain words

The finder bar takes a sentence like “I need help with my garden” and shows the five closest files, with a button to jump straight to each.

Dial pad

Dial any 11-digit Signature number to open that AI: district 200 = Signature systems, 300 = persona sims, 4xx = field blocks, /* SL-TWINS: dialhelp */ 500 = word AIs, 600 = Signature-Line twins. 500 = word AIs. Clear wipes the number, ⌫ removes one digit, and a full 11 digits dials by itself. A number with no listing gets a prank call — a surprise AI picks up anyway.

Talk to any AI

Tap any listing to open its file, then type in the chat and press Send (Enter works). Replies are generated by Signature Llama v2 when its weights load, otherwise the JAHtalk engine — every reply says which engine answered. Tick “Read replies aloud” to hear them.

Read aloud, copy, download

Every file has Read aloud (tiered speech: on-device voice first, then web voices), Copy, and Download .txt / .py. Downloads carry a content hash so you can tell the file you saved matches the published one. The RUNTIME & LIMITATIONS block on each file says honestly what runs where.

Group call

“➕ Add to call” on any file puts that AI on a group call (up to 8). Say something once and each AI answers in turn, in its own voice.

Genome Lab

Build your own AI: pick one strength for each of 10 functions (or load a preset), name it, and press Forge. Forging needs at least one gene block — the lab says so instead of failing silently. Every forged AI gets a deterministic ID (same genes, same ID) and a shareable link: Copy link gives an address that re-creates the identical AI on any device.

Mix Lab

Pick two AIs and press Fuse them to make a hybrid with its own chat. Hybrids are deterministic: hybrid #424242 is the same fusion for everyone, and #hybrid-JAH-AI-MIX-424242 is its address.

Auto-Forge

March to a million hybrids: fresh deterministic fusions on every visit, or dial any number from 1 to 1,000,000 directly. The counter tracks how many you have opened on this device.

Arena

Pit two AIs against each other: a chat bout (1–20 rounds on your topic) or a versus test where both answer the same question side by side. Read the bout aloud, or copy either answer.

Professional Visits

Book a phone visit with a professional AI — doctors, lawyers, mechanics, chefs and more. Each visit is a warm appointment-style consultation. Medical, legal and financial AIs always carry their “not a licensed professional” warning, on the card, in the visit, and in their files.

Sister-site lines & word AIs

Sister-site lines ring AIs that live on Manon’s other sites. Word AIs give every dictionary word its own AI (district 500), loaded on demand.

Deep links

Every record has an address: #file-ID for book AIs, #hybrid-JAH-AI-MIX-###### for hybrids, #gen-STAMP for forged AIs, #wordai-STAMP for word AIs, or ?dial=ID in the address bar.
